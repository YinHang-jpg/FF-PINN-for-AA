FF-PINN minimal example — expected output
==========================================

Command (repository root):

    python review/examples/run_example.py

The example calls `results/PD_vs_time/PD_time.run_single_simulation` for
0.01 s of physical time (10 000 steps, dt = 1e-6 s), compares KDE relative
concentration change to bundled DEM and FEM (COMSOL) references, and writes

    review/examples/density_comparison_t0.01s.png

This demo plot does not overwrite manuscript figures under `figures/`.

Representative stdout (hardware-dependent wall times):

Notes
-----
- Relative-change magnitudes may vary slightly with PyTorch / CUDA versions.
- Peak locations at 10 kHz should remain near the standing-wave nodes
  (~8.5 mm and ~25.5 mm spacing of λ/2 ≈ 17 mm).
- If `PINN/` is incomplete, the script trains the five sub-networks first
  unless `--skip-train` is passed.
